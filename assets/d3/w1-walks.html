Interactive · Every entry of Ak is a walk count you can list

The proposition, made checkable. Each cell of Ak holds a count, and the walks it counts are drawn and named beside it, so you can verify the claim rather than trust it. Try this: at k = 2, click a diagonal cell and watch the count equal that node's degree, because a two-step walk that returns home is exactly a step out to a neighbour and back. Then click an off-diagonal cell: the walks listed all pass through a shared neighbour, which is why that entry counts common neighbours. Finally switch to k = 3 and watch the matrix fill in — three steps reach almost everyone, which is the small-world warning of §2 arriving as arithmetic, and the reason a three-layer network in Week 6 is far less local than it sounds.
