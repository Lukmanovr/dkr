Interactive · The random surfer, one step at a time

Power iteration is the surfer's routine repeated: follow a random outlink with probability β, teleport otherwise, and the panel writes exactly that update with the current β and teleport vector t filled in. Watch three things move together: the arrows, whose width is the mass each link carries this step; the discs and their numbers; and the chart of the total change per step, which falls by roughly a factor β each iteration — the contraction of the proposition, seen rather than proved. Try this: (1) press step until the change drops below the dashed 10−3 line and read off how many steps it took against the bound printed underneath; (2) drag β to 0, step once, and explain why every page now shows the same 0.100; (3) drag it to 0.99 and watch the chart flatten — a weak contraction crawls; (4) tick the personalized box, reset, and step to convergence: every teleport now lands on Q, so rank pools around Q and the pages it reaches — that checkbox is how "recommendations near this user" works.
