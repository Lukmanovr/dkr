Interactive · What xTLx actually counts

The quadratic form has no mystery in it: each edge contributes the square of the gap between its two endpoints, and xTLx is the sum. All three presets use the same six values, three of each sign, so only their arrangement on the graph changes and the chance average stays fixed at 16.8 — which is what makes "small" meaningful rather than arbitrary. Try this: click people to flip sides and drive the total to zero. You can only do it by putting everyone on one side, which is the precise sense in which the Laplacian is blind to constant signals: it measures differences, and a constant has none. Then notice what the middle preset shows — an arrangement can look untidy and still sit at chance, so eyeballing a drawing is not evidence, and the number is.
