Interactive · Neighbor sampling: pay less, accept noise

GraphSAGE's bargain, quantified on a real hub: aggregate K sampled neighbors instead of all of them — constant cost per node, at the price of estimator variance. Try this: park on node 33 (17 neighbors), hammer resample at K = 2 and watch the estimate swing; raise K and watch the spread column shrink like 1/√K. Then visit node 8 (degree 3) and explain why sampling buys nothing there — and why, on a heavy-tailed graph (Week 1), that asymmetry is exactly the point.
